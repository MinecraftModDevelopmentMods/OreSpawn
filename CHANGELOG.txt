* Apply underwater materials from the corrected ground and ceiling materials to roof undersides.
* Preserve trees, vegetation, structures and block entities by running surface replacement before late features.
* Preserve exact custom-biome rules and stagger close Stable Layers geome boundaries.
* Recalibrate Edge Detail and the managed vanilla-ore defaults for Minecraft 1.14.4.
* Remove later-version basalt, Nether-gold, ancient-debris, and biome defaults from fresh 1.14.4 profiles.
* Support the single 1.14.4 dimension fluid while retaining later deep-aquifer profile fields as inactive data.
* Adapt static biome features and set-based biome providers to Forge 28 without changing public contracts.
* Rebuild vanilla spring leaves with a Forge 28-native wrapper so provider rocks work without mutating unavailable platform fields.
* Create the public ore-pattern registry through Forge 28's native registry lifecycle and retain external registrations.
* Restore reliable 1.14.4 editor backgrounds, tooltip clearing, and translated cycle-button captions.

* Existing chunks are not rewritten; the correction applies while generating new chunks.
