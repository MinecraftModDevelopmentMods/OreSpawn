  scan before structure and vegetation features can author matching blocks.

  saved profiles remain unchanged.

* Fix provider top and filler materials being generated one block below exposed ground.
* Apply underwater materials from the corrected ground and ceiling materials to roof undersides.
* Preserve trees, vegetation, structures and block entities by running surface replacement before late features.
* Preserve exact datapack-biome rules and stagger close Stable Layers geome boundaries.
* Recalibrate Edge Detail and the managed vanilla-ore defaults for Minecraft 1.17.1.
* Support the single 1.17 dimension fluid while retaining later deep-aquifer profile fields as inactive data.
* Existing chunks are not rewritten; the correction applies while generating new chunks.
